Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. Your climb needs no account and no server of ours; only the optional Friends tab keeps your display name and what you choose to share in Firebase. Your data is included in your normal iPhone backup. You can export a copy at any time from Profile, Export and backup, Save a copy, which opens the share sheet with a file you can save or send.

I missed a day. Is my streak gone?

No. One missed day costs some XP, not the streak. The streak only breaks after two missed days in a row. You also get one planned camp day, a rest day, each week that keeps the streak intact. You can move it from Home or change which weekday it falls on in Profile.

My reminders are not firing

Open iOS Settings, find the app and check that notifications are allowed. Then open the app once so it can refresh its schedule. Reminders follow the times and windows you set in Profile. If you travel across time zones or work nights, set your day start hour in Profile so days roll over when you expect them to.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, tap Subscriptions and choose the app. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for. Cancelling during the free trial means you are never charged. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Open Profile inside the app and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple account you used to subscribe.

Can I change my plan, program or difficulty?

Yes, from day one. Profile lets you change the program, the difficulty, the camp day, the day start hour and your reminder times. You can also add custom quests, reorder quests and skip a quest with a reason from Home. Changing these does not reset your progress.

How do I start over?

Profile contains Reset challenge. It is free, keeps your subscription and starts the same plan again from day 1. To build a different plan, use Change program in Profile. If you want everything gone, use Delete all data instead. That removes every record from your phone and returns you to the welcome screen. Your purchase status stays, so the app still knows you subscribed. There is no copy anywhere else, so there is no undo.

The widget is not updating

Widgets refresh when you open the app or check off a quest. If a widget looks stale, open the app once. If it stays stale, remove the widget and add it again from the Home Screen or Lock Screen.

How do I report someone in Friends, or delete my Friends account?

Every post and comment by another person has a More button with Report and Block. We review reports within 24 hours. To leave Friends, open Profile and tap Delete Friends account, or use Delete account in your profile inside the Friends tab. It removes your name, your friend list and every post, reaction and comment you made. You can also write to us at the address above.

Is this medical advice?

No. The app is a daily habit and routine tool. Quests are simple everyday actions. It does not diagnose or treat anything. If you have a health concern, talk to a qualified professional.
